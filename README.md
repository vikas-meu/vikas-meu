## 🌼 About

I build robots end to end — mechanical design, electronics, firmware and the vision/AI stack on top — and take them from a sketch on paper to a working machine in front of judges. My work spans autonomous field robots, teleoperated humanoids and vision-guided multi-robot systems.

- 🎓 &nbsp;B.Tech, Electronics & Telecommunication — **SSIPMT, Raipur**
- 🔧 &nbsp;Focus: **ROS2, computer vision, embedded AI on edge devices** (Jetson, Raspberry Pi, ESP32)
- 🌼 &nbsp;Every robot I build carries a small daisy somewhere on it
